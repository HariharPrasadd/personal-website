SoC requirements

Requirement Target
Concurrent cameras (>= 4) simultaneous streams
Camera type MIPI CSI-2
Eye cameras 2 simultaneous low-latency mono/global-shutter streams
World cameras 2 simultaneous RGB streams with ISP support, synchronised stereo pair
Stereo synchronization Hardware-trigger or tightly synchronized capture for world-camera pair
Camera timestamping Accurate per-frame timestamps
IMU timestamping Accurate high-rate timestamps synchronized with camera timebase if possible
CSI capacity Enough CSI receivers / lanes / virtual-channel support for all 4 cameras
ISP capacity At least 2 simultaneous RGB ISP pipelines; eye cameras can ideally bypass heavy ISP
Display At least 1 MIPI DSI
AI acceleration Preferably \(\ge 10\) TOPS for long-term architecture
GPU Vulkan/OpenGL-class
CPU Modern multi-core ARM64
RAM Prefer 8 GB development configuration
Memory bandwidth High enough for 4 camera streams + GPU/NPU workloads concurrently
Storage eMMC/UFS
Bluetooth BLE 5.x usable from OS; must support acting as BLE central for ring
Wi-Fi Wi-Fi 6+
USB USB 3 preferred
IMU interface At least one usable SPI or I²C controller; SPI preferred if high-rate/low-latency sampling is needed
GPIO / PWM Enough GPIO for camera reset/power-down, sync/trigger, NIR LED control, interrupts
OS Linux strongly preferred
Camera framework Drivers/BSP must support simultaneous capture from all 4 sensors
Hardware video Hardware encode/decode useful for logging/streaming world-camera feeds
Power Mobile/embedded class
DVFS / low-power states Required; fine-grained power management strongly preferred
Thermal envelope Suitable for battery-powered wearable use
Development hardware Must actually be obtainable in low quantity
Camera drivers Extremely important
Documentation Must be usable without impossible NDA barriers
Product lifetime Prefer long-lifecycle embedded part
Dev-kit exposure Dev kit must physically expose enough CSI/DSI/GPIO interfaces to test the full V0
